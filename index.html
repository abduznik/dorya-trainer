DORYATRAINER

Electric Wind God Fist · just-frame practice

Keyboard: WASD to move, K is button 2 (J=1, L=3, ;=4) · Controller: d-pad / stick, Triangle / Y is 2 · Mouse: camera only

Electric on keyboard: tap D, release, press S, then add D and hit K on the same instant.

Electric Wind God Fist

The EWGF is the Mishima launcher: f, n, d, d/f+2. It is "electric" only when button 2 is pressed on the exact same frame that the stick reaches d/f. One frame late and you get a regular Wind God Fist. The game runs at 60 frames per second, so the window is 16.7 ms. The move comes out in 11 frames, and the electric launches on hit.

CD, f, n, d, d/f …wavedash: tap f to cancel each crouch dash into the next

2 on the d/f frame of any crouch dash is the electric. 2 later during the crouch dash is a regular WGF.

Perfect Electric (PEWGF)

The mist-step version: f, n, d/f+2 with no d in between, 2 on the exact frame d/f is reached. It is one input frame faster than the normal electric because there is no d frame to pass through, which is what makes 1-frame links like ws2 into electric possible. If the 2 is late on this path you simply get d/f+2, not a Wind God Fist. The trainer counts it as an electric and shows it separately.
